Privacy Policy for MycoJournal Mobile

This Privacy Policy explains how MycoJournal Mobile accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares data when you use the Android or iOS mobile app.

Overview

MycoJournal Mobile is a mushroom cultivation and foraging journal. The app lets signed-in users keep grow records, logs, harvests, foraging sessions, mushroom-find records, notes, settings, and optional photos. Some features also use your device location when you choose to enable location access.

This page is intended to support the public privacy-policy requirement for MycoJournal Mobile on Google Play and to describe the app’s current implemented data flows.

Data We Access and Why

Google sign-in and account identity

What is involved: Google account details needed to authenticate you with Firebase, such as your name, email address, and account identifier.

Why the app uses it: To sign you into your MycoJournal workspace and associate your saved journal data with your account.

Who processes or receives it: Google and Firebase Authentication process this information to provide sign-in.

Journal and app content

What is involved: Grow records, logs, harvests, foraging sessions, mushroom finds, notes, settings, timestamps, and related metadata that you create while signed in.

Why the app uses it: To save and sync your journal history and app settings across sessions.

Who processes or receives it: Stored in Firebase Firestore.

Photos you choose to upload

What is involved: Photos attached to grows, harvests, and foraging entries, plus related metadata such as file size and dimensions.

Why the app uses it: To let you document grows, harvests, and wild finds inside the app.

Who processes or receives it: Uploaded to Cloudinary. Photo deletion requests are routed through the app’s authenticated Netlify function.

Location data

What is involved: Approximate or precise location, coordinates, accuracy, and place labels when you use location-based foraging features or choose your current location.

Why the app uses it: To log foraging sessions and mushroom find spots, provide place labels, and fetch local weather context for those entries.

Who processes or receives it: Used with Nominatim for reverse geocoding, Open-Meteo for weather lookups, and Google Maps features or links when map functionality is used.

Location-Specific Disclosures

MycoJournal Mobile requests foreground location access only for user-facing foraging features, such as capturing your current location for a foraging session or mushroom find, generating a place label from saved coordinates, and fetching weather for that location.

The app does not use location for advertising. If you do not grant location permission, you can still use the app, but location-based foraging and weather features may be limited or unavailable.

Third-Party Services

  • Google / Firebase for authentication and Firestore-hosted app data.
  • Cloudinary for user-selected photo storage and delivery.
  • Netlify for the authenticated backend function used by photo deletion.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) for reverse geocoding coordinates into place labels.
  • Open-Meteo for weather lookups based on selected coordinates.
  • Google Maps services when map previews, map links, or related map features are used.

How Data Is Stored and Protected

MycoJournal Mobile uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Firebase Firestore for account-linked journal data. Based on the current implementation, Firestore access is restricted to the authenticated user’s own records. Photo deletion requests are sent through an authenticated backend endpoint rather than an open public delete call.

Data sent between the app and service providers is transmitted over network connections provided by those services. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but the app is designed to use authenticated access controls for account-linked data.

Retention and Deletion

Journal records and settings associated with your signed-in account are retained until they are deleted through the app or otherwise removed from the underlying project data. Uploaded photos remain associated with the related entry unless they are deleted. Temporary logs, backups, or provider-side operational records may persist for limited periods under the service providers’ own retention practices.

If you need help with a deletion request that you cannot complete from within the app, use the developer contact method listed on the Google Play listing for MycoJournal Mobile.

Children’s Privacy

MycoJournal Mobile is not designed as a children’s app. If you believe personal data was provided to the app inappropriately, use the developer contact method listed on the Google Play listing for MycoJournal Mobile.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as MycoJournal Mobile changes. When the policy is updated, the last-updated date on this page will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests related to MycoJournal Mobile, use the developer contact information provided on the app’s Google Play listing.
